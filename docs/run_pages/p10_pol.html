They converge to the same navigator. Both walk a path within 4–7% of the straight line. The learned arm reaches the goal in a third fewer steps purely because each step is longer — not because it steers better. Mid-training this looked very different: at u1050 the frozen arm was at 1.27× optimal against the learned arm's 1.68×, and the gap closed entirely over the following 950 updates.

The frozen arm is better under distractors. 0.958 against 0.927 success at ten, and a shorter path (11.64 against 13.78 cells). So pinning the speed costs steps and buys robustness — which is the trade to weigh before P6 asks one model to do both regimes.
