# Audit of the edits from four new papers

Audit date 2026-11-20. Every claim below was checked against the PDFs in `papers/new/` and the Kjelstrup et al. 2008 main text, not against the notes in `fulltext_new_papers.md`. Figure values come from my own reading. Fig. S6 of the Kjelstrup supplement is vector art, so I read its marker coordinates directly. Fig. 9 of Terrazas et al. is a raster image, so I traced the curve pixels against the axis lines. Scripts and renders are in the job's `tmp/audit_new/` folder. Line numbers refer to `docs/LITERATURE_PLACE_FIELDS_POPULATION_SIMILARITY.md` as of this audit.

Verdicts are confirmed, corrected or unverifiable. "Corrected" means the review text needs a change, given in the last column.
