# Open datasets of spatially tuned hippocampal and related cells with position tracking

Scope note. I read landing pages and documentation only and downloaded no data. "Verified" means I read the fact on the dataset's landing page or the repository's metadata API. "From memory" means I did not confirm it on a page this session, and the writer should check it before relying on it. Download status was checked on 2026-11-15.

## Which public datasets exist?

### Takeaway
Most fully public hippocampal datasets with position are on CRCNS, which needs a free account, and on DANDI, which is open and uses NWB. A few more sit on Figshare, Zenodo and NIRD. Of the large-environment studies named in the brief, only Rich et al. 2014 (48 m track, CRCNS hc-31) and Tanni et al. 2022 (up to 3.5 x 2.5 m, UCL Figshare) have full spike and position data public. Eliav et al. 2021 (bats) and Harland et al. 2021 posted only example-cell or figure data. I found no public deposit for any of the five subiculum papers or for human single-unit navigation.

### Cited findings

CRCNS hippocampus (hc) series, index page
- CRCNS lists hc-1 through hc-31. The ones with behaving animals and position are hc-2, hc-3, hc-5, hc-6, hc-11, hc-12, hc-13, hc-14, hc-16, hc-17, hc-18, hc-19, hc-20, hc-21, hc-22, hc-23, hc-27, hc-28, hc-29, hc-30 and hc-31. hc-12 and hc-21 are macaque hippocampus during virtual navigation. The index page mentions no subiculum dataset. [CRCNS hc index](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc)

hc-3, Buzsáki lab, multi-region, verified
- Long-Evans rats, 11 animals, 442 sessions, 204.5 h. The landing page gives 7,737 neurons. Regions are CA1, CA3, DG and EC layers 2 to 5. Recordings used 31 to 127 channels at 20 kHz. The data include head position, head direction and, for many sessions, video. [CRCNS hc-3 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-3/about-hc-3)
- The total is about 433 GB compressed, in one tar.gz per session. Download needs a CRCNS account. The data are also on the Buzsáki lab website. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K09G5JRZ. [CRCNS hc-3 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-3/about-hc-3)
- Environments and session counts from the F1000Research data paper are as follows. bigSquare is a 180 x 180 cm open field with 91 sessions. bigSquarePlus is the same box with plus-shaped walls, 2 sessions. midSquare is 120 x 120 cm, 14 sessions. "Open" is 100 x 200 cm, 3 sessions. The linear track is 250 x 7 cm, 119 sessions. linearOne and linearTwo are a 170 cm track placed at two room locations, 8 sessions each. Mwheel is a 100 x 120 cm T-maze with a wheel delay, 89 sessions. There are also a T-maze, a plus maze, a 100 x 200 cm zigzag maze, wheel running and sleep. [Mizuseki et al. 2014, F1000Research](https://f1000research.com/articles/3-98/v2)
- Cell counts by region are CA1 3,724, CA3 1,123, DG 135 and EC 2,622, for 7,736 cells in total. The paper does not report a typical number of simultaneously recorded units. It says to find that in the metadata tables (CSV, Excel, SQLite). [Mizuseki et al. 2014](https://f1000research.com/articles/3-98/v2)
- Terms of use: scientific use only, no redistribution except to direct collaborators or students, credit the contributors and CRCNS, and cite the original papers. [Mizuseki et al. 2014](https://f1000research.com/articles/3-98/v2)
- Papers to cite are Mizuseki K, Sirota A, Pastalkova E, Buzsáki G (2009), "Theta oscillations provide temporal windows for local circuit computation in the entorhinal-hippocampal loop," Neuron 64(2):267-280, and Diba K, Buzsáki G (2008), "Hippocampal network dynamics constrain the time lag between pyramidal cells across modified environments," J Neurosci 28(50):13448-13456. The third is Mizuseki K, Diba K, Pastalkova E, Teeters J, Sirota A, Buzsáki G (2014), "Neurosharing: large-scale data sets (spike, LFP) recorded from the hippocampal-entorhinal system in behaving rats," F1000Research 3:98, doi:10.12688/f1000research.3895.2. [CRCNS hc-3 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-3/about-hc-3). The Neuron 2009 DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2009.08.037 and the J Neurosci 2008 DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3824-08.2008 are from memory.

hc-2, Buzsáki lab, CA1 open field, verified
- 3 Long-Evans rats, 21 sessions lasting about 17 min to 1 h 46 min, about 19 h in total. Recordings come from right dorsal CA1 on 31 to 64 channels. Rats foraged on an elevated square platform whose dimensions the page does not give. The data are about 136 GB compressed. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K0Z60KZ9. Cite Mizuseki et al. 2009, Neuron 64(2):267-80. [CRCNS hc-2 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-2/about-hc-2)

hc-5, Pastalkova, verified
- One rat, left and right CA1 plus right EC, 125-channel silicon probe. Tasks are left/right alternation, wheel running and platform exploration. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K0KS6PHF. Cite Pastalkova et al. 2008, Science 321(5894), "Internally generated cell assembly sequences in the rat hippocampus." [CRCNS hc-5 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-5/about-hc-5). The paper DOI 10.1126/science.1159775 is from memory.

hc-6, Frank lab, two W-tracks, verified
- 9 male Long-Evans rats. Recordings are CA1 and CA3, or MEC and CA1, during alternation on two W-tracks that are geometrically identical but visually distinct. The data are MAT files of position, spikes, LFP and task, about 22 GB compressed. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K0NK3BZJ. The page lists four papers: Karlsson & Frank 2008, J Neurosci 28(52):14271-14281, "Network dynamics underlying the formation of sparse, informative representations in the hippocampus"; Karlsson & Frank 2009, Nat Neurosci 12(7):913-918, "Awake replay of remote experiences in the hippocampus"; Carr, Karlsson & Frank 2012, Neuron 75:700-713; and Singer et al. 2013, Neuron 77(6):1163-1173. The page gives no track dimensions or cell counts. [CRCNS hc-6 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-6/about-hc-5). The DOIs 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.4261-08.2008 and 10.1038/nn.2344 are from memory.

hc-11 and DANDI:000044, Grosmark & Buzsáki, verified
- 4 male Long-Evans rats, 8 bilateral silicon-probe CA1 sessions. Each session is about 4 h of PRE sleep, about 45 min on a novel maze, and about 4 h of POST sleep. The mazes are a 1.6 m wooden linear track, a 1 m diameter circular platform, or a 2 m metal linear track. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K0862DC5. [CRCNS hc-11 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-11/about-hc-11)
- The same data are on DANDI as NWB: 8 files, about 73.4 GB, CC-BY-4.0, open access. Papers are Grosmark AD, Buzsáki G (2016), "Diversity in neural firing dynamics supports both rigid and learned hippocampal sequences," Science 351:1440-1443, doi:10.1126/science.aad1935, and Chen Z et al. (2016), Sci Rep 6:32193, doi:10.1038/srep32193. [DANDI 000044 metadata](https://api.dandiarchive.org/api/dandisets/000044/versions/draft/)

hc-13, Frank lab, verified
- Rats, CA1 plus dorsal mPFC, foraging task with alternating choices. About 8 GB. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K0H41PK8. Paper: Yu JY, Kay K, Liu D, et al. (2017), "Distinct hippocampal-cortical memory representations for experiences associated with movement versus immobility," eLife 6, doi:10.7554/eLife.27621. [CRCNS hc-13 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-13/about-hc-13)

hc-16 and hc-17, Fenton lab, verified
- hc-16 has CA1 tetrode recordings from wild-type and Fmr1-null mice in a small box, a large box, a ring and a circular open field, stationary or rotating. About 500 MB in a custom binary format. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K01V5C44. The page cites Talbot et al. (Neuron, bioRxiv doi:10.1101/152462) and Dvorak et al. (PLoS Biology, bioRxiv doi:10.1101/152488). [CRCNS hc-16 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-16/about-hc-16)

Paper: van Dijk MT, Fenton AA, "On how the dentate gyrus contributes to memory discrimination," Neuron (bioRxiv 235473). [CRCNS hc-17 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-17/about-hc-17). The published DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2018.04.018 is from memory.

hc-22, Jercog et al., three environments, verified
- 12 male C57BL/6J mice, 1,326 CA1 neurons, 4 tetrodes per drive. There are three environments, each about 50 cm wide with 40 cm walls: a square with black walls, a circle with a brown textured floor, and a white circle. Each environment got four 10 min sessions per day over 7 to 10 days. MATLAB, about 715 MB. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K09W0CP7. Paper: Jercog PE, Ahmadian Y, Woodruff C, Deb-Sen R, Abbott LF, Kandel ER, "Heading direction with respect to a reference point modulates place-cell activity," bioRxiv doi:10.1101/433516. The page dates it 2019 and bioRxiv dates it 2018. [CRCNS hc-22 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-22/about-hc-22)

hc-23, Colgin lab, MEC grid cells on Figshare, verified
- 6 rats, MEC layer II/III, 1 to 7 sessions per rat. Each session is three 20 min bouts in a 100 x 100 cm open field plus an overnight recording in a 60 x 60 cm box. MATLAB and Neuralynx files. The files are on Figshare, a free download with no license stated on the page. Dataset DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.c.4496375. Paper: Trettel SG, Trimper JB, Hwaun E, Fiete IR, Colgin LL (2019), Nat Neurosci 22(4):609-617. [CRCNS hc-23 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-23/about-hc-23). The title "Grid cell co-activity patterns during sleep reflect spatial overlap of grid fields during active behaviors" and DOI 10.1038/s41593-019-0359-6 are from memory.

hc-29, Wirtshafter & Wilson, CA1 plus lateral septum, verified
- Rats, 16 tetrodes in CA1 and 16 in dorsal lateral septum, double-sided T-maze. About 2.3 GB of MATLAB. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K0NG4NV8. The page asks users to consult H. Wirtshafter before publishing. Paper: Wirtshafter HS, Wilson MA (2020), "Differences in reward biased spatial representations in the lateral septum and hippocampus." [CRCNS hc-29 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-29/about-hc-29). The journal, eLife 9:e55252, and DOI 10.7554/eLife.55252 are from memory.

hc-30, Jercog & Schnitzer, CA1 calcium imaging, verified
- 12 mice, 110 sessions, 27,898 CA1 neurons, 151 to 497 per session with a median of 235. Mice ran a 120 cm linear track for about 20 min, 150 to 380 runs per session. The data are calcium events in one MATLAB file of about 136 MB. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K0GH9G5X. The page asks users to consult the contributors before publishing. Paper: Hazon O, Minces VH, Tomàs DP, Ganguli S, Schnitzer MJ, Jercog PE (2022), "Noise correlations in neural ensemble activity limit the accuracy of hippocampal spatial representations." [CRCNS hc-30 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-30/about-hc-30). The journal, Nat Commun 13:4276, and DOI 10.1038/s41467-022-31254-y are from memory.

hc-31, Rich, Liaw & Lee 2014, 48 m track, verified
- 5 rats, dorsal CA1 tetrodes, single units plus LFP. Rats explored a novel 48 m linear track, repeated the track exposure, explored a second, smaller open field, and slept before and after. MATLAB and Neuralynx .ncs files, about 8.4 GB compressed. Dataset DOI 10.6080/K0BV7DT2 (2022). [CRCNS hc-31 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-31/about-hc-31)
- The usage condition is unusual: work using the data should list P. Dylan Rich and Albert K. Lee as authors, and users should contact them first. [CRCNS hc-31 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-31/about-hc-31)
- Paper: Rich PD, Liaw HP, Lee AK (2014), "Large environments reveal the statistical structure governing hippocampal representations," Science 345(6198):814-817, doi:10.1126/science.1255635. [CRCNS hc-31 about](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-31/about-hc-31)

## Which datasets support field statistics, population similarity over distance, and remapping?

### Takeaway
For (b), population-vector similarity as a function of distance across a whole large environment, the best public candidates are Tanni et al. 2022 (CA1, 2D, up to 8.75 m², 89 to 172 place cells per rat) and CRCNS hc-31 (CA1, 1D, 48 m track). hc-3 bigSquare sessions (1.8 x 1.8 m) are a third option with high channel counts. Imaging sets such as hc-30, DANDI 002030 and DANDI 002047 have hundreds of simultaneous cells, but only in arenas 1.2 m or smaller. For (c), remapping, the strongest public sets are Alme 2014 (11 rooms), hc-22 (3 arenas), hc-6 (2 W-tracks), Tanni 2022 (4 arenas of different sizes) and Plitt & Giocomo (morphed VR tracks).

### Inferences
