Place-cell populations

Build a population from random place fields and watch its similarity kernel. Space is a 128 × 128-cell square whose edges wrap, so every place has the same surroundings.

Population

Each field is a Gaussian bump with peak 1 at a random place; width is its full width at half maximum, drawn from a log-normal: the median stays where its slider puts it, and spread (the standard deviation of log width) widens the distribution around it, which raises the mean. Each cell's number of fields is Poisson. Changing one setting keeps every other draw, so maps change smoothly.

Similarity

The similarity of two places is the cosine between the population's rate vectors there, averaged over every place. "After removing the mean" first subtracts one constant, the population's mean rate, from every cell (global inhibition), which removes the floor that overlapping fields create.
